## Who can apply

Members of the Jamaat and their dependent family members who need help with hospital treatment, tests or medicines.

## Documents required

1. Jamaat membership card of the patient or the head of the family.
2. Doctor's prescription or referral letter, not older than [30] days.
3. Hospital estimate or bills for the treatment.
4. Recent test reports related to the treatment.
5. Income proof of the family.

## How to apply

1. Upload the prescription or referral letter in the Medical section of Wasila.
2. Check the details Wasila reads from the document and correct anything that is wrong.
3. Choose an available appointment slot for the matching department.
4. The Medical Committee reviews the request and the office confirms the appointment.

## Which office handles it

The Medical Committee at the Jamaat office, [office address], [office hours].

## Emergencies

In an emergency, go to the nearest hospital or call 112 (108 for an ambulance) first. Apply for assistance afterwards.
