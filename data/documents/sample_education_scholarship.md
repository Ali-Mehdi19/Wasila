## Who can apply

Students from Jamaat member families enrolled in a recognised school, college or university course.

## Documents required

1. Jamaat membership card of the student or the head of the family.
2. Marksheet of the last examination passed.
3. Admission letter or bonafide certificate from the college.
4. Fee receipt or fee structure for the current year.
5. Income proof of the family.

## How to apply

1. Open the Scholarship section of Wasila. Your personal and family details are filled in from your profile.
2. Upload your marksheet and fee receipt. Course, college, marks and fees are read automatically; check them.
3. Submit the application. The Education Committee reviews it and you can track it in My Requests.
